# Editorial and AI Newsroom Policy

RundaTech uses AI to accelerate research, drafting, classification, summarization, internal linking, and distribution. AI is an assistant, not the publisher of record.

## Editorial principles
- Accuracy before speed.
- Primary sources preferred for material factual claims.
- Discovery is not verification.
- Rumors, leaks, estimates, and allegations must be clearly labeled.
- Corrections must be transparent and retain revision history.
- Sponsored/commercial relationships must not silently alter editorial conclusions.
- Articles should add RundaTech context, data, analysis, or reporting rather than paraphrase another publisher.

## AI permissions
AI may gather approved-source evidence, extract claims, compare sources, identify contradictions, draft structured copy, suggest headlines, propose SEO metadata, generate social variants, and flag legal/editorial risk.

AI must not fabricate quotes, interviews, eyewitness reporting, sources, product specs, prices, funding amounts, dates, or attribution. Unknown values remain unknown.

## Verification states
Suggested states: `discovered`, `unverified`, `source_verified`, `cross_checked`, `human_verified`, `disputed`, `published`, `corrected`, `retracted`.

## Source hierarchy
Prefer, where applicable: official/regulatory/filing/direct source -> first-party company/investor source -> reputable specialist/news source -> authorized partner/retailer -> aggregator/marketplace/social/user submission. The hierarchy is contextual, not absolute; conflicts must be investigated.

## Sensitive coverage
Stories involving allegations, legal disputes, personal safety, security vulnerabilities, market-moving claims, confidential material, or significant reputational risk require heightened review. AI may assist but should not autonomously publish.

## Corrections
When a material error is found: preserve the original revision, verify corrected evidence, publish the correction/update note where appropriate, update linked structured data, regenerate derived summaries/social assets if necessary, and retain audit attribution.
