# Country Launch Checklist

Before enabling a new RundaTech market/domain:

## Domain and routing
- domain registered and DNS/TLS configured
- hostname mapped to correct `market_id`
- canonical/hreflang strategy reviewed
- redirect behavior tested

## Market configuration
- country/currency/locale/timezone configured
- local legal/privacy/consumer notices reviewed
- price formatting/tax assumptions documented
- market-scoped staff permissions configured

## Commercial data
- retailers registered and verified
- offer/product mapping quality sampled
- price freshness/anomaly thresholds configured
- affiliate disclosure/relationship handling configured where applicable

## Product/content
- global canonical products reused rather than duplicated
- local availability/warranty/import notes separated from global technical facts
- localized editorial content adds real market value rather than cloning pages

## Operations
- market dashboards/alerts enabled
- support ownership defined
- crawl/feed rate limits verified
- rollback/disable-market procedure tested

A country launch must be reversible through configuration without deleting global product records.
